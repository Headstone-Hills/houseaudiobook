Chapter One. The Fox and the Grapes.

A hungry fox saw some fine bunches of grapes hanging from a vine. The vine was trained along a high trellis. He did his best to reach them by jumping as high as he could.

[p. 2]
But it was all in vain, for they were just out of reach. So he gave up trying, and walked away with an air of dignity. I thought those grapes were ripe, he said, but I see now they are quite sour.

[p. 3]
Chapter Two. The Tortoise and the Hare.

A hare once made fun of a tortoise for being so slow. The tortoise laughed and said that he would win a race against him.

The hare soon ran far ahead, and lay down to take a nap. The tortoise plodded on and never stopped. When the hare woke up, the tortoise was already at the finish line.
